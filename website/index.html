What's inside

Ragdolls & gore

Happy-Wheels-style falls: limbs come off, oil and bolts fly. You can turn it down or off.

Multiplayer

Public and private servers, friends, chat and a leaderboard, with no IP addresses to share.

Lua scripting

Roblox-style scripts: Touched, tools, leaderstats, saved data and more.

Real physics

Tumbling parts, ropes, springs, hinges, motors and welds.

Tools & hotbar

Give players bats, guns or anything else, equipped with 1–9.

Your own look

Hats, shirts and pants from the catalog, paid for with Bolts you earn by playing.

Decals & audio

Upload pictures and sounds on the Create page and use them in your games.

Roblox files

Import and export .rbxl and .rbxm places and models.

Make your own with Studio

Studio looks and works like Roblox Studio: Explorer, Properties, a script editor with autocomplete, and ready-made pieces like spawns, checkpoints and traps.

Press Play to test straight away. Build with friends in Team Create, then publish your game to the server so everyone can play it.

The site: in the app and on the web

The Guts&Bolts Player feels like the classic 2011 site: a games list, a catalog, friends, groups, a Create page and daily Bolts.

It works on phones too. The Android app plays every game with touch controls.

You can also use it right here in your browser: sign in to browse games, buy from the catalog, upload decals and audio, manage your games, and chat with friends and groups.

Join a server

Open the Player, click Offline (or Online) in the menu bar, and type the server address you were given. Then sign up, pick a game and press Play.
